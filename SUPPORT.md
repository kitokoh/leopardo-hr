# Support Guide

If you're looking for help with Leopardo RH, here are the best ways to get assistance.

## 📚 Documentation
Before seeking support, please check our comprehensive documentation:
- [Quick Start Guide](QUICKSTART.md)
- [Architecture Overview](ARCHITECTURE.md)
- [Deployment Guide](docs/deployment/DEPLOYMENT_GUIDE.md)
- [FAQ & Knowledge Base](docs/README.md)

## 💬 Community Support
- **GitHub Issues:** For bug reports and feature requests, please use our [Issue Tracker](https://github.com/your-org/leopardo-rh/issues).
- **Discussions:** Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/your-org/leopardo-rh/discussions) for general questions, ideas, and troubleshooting.
- **Discord:** Join our [Community Discord](https://discord.gg/leopardo-rh) for real-time chat with other developers and users.

## 🛠 Professional Support
If you need enterprise-grade support, custom feature development, or integration assistance:
- **Email:** Contact our professional services team at `hello@leopardo-rh.com`.
- **Website:** Visit [leopardo-rh.com](https://leopardo-rh.com) for more information.

## 🛡️ Security Support
If you've found a security vulnerability, please refer to our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) for reporting instructions. Do not open public issues for security flaws.
