Library: the catalogue, the access, the reading

🏭 Verticals16:9 ⏱ 60 s 🎯 Training institutions, companies, organisations that publish resources

Film objective

Show the Library module: a catalogue of digital resources (books, ebooks, guides, manuals, reports, articles), access models, per-unit purchase, reading progress and semantic search.

Hook (first 3 seconds)

« A resource catalogue that lives in a spreadsheet always ends up lying. »

Breakdown

TimeVoice-over Shot / imageOn-screen text
0:00-0:08 A resource catalogue that lives in a spreadsheet always ends up lying. Bureau encombre de catalogues papier et d'un tableur ouvert. FR The catalogue that drifts.
0:08-0:22 The Library holds the catalogue: books, ebooks, guides, manuals, reports, articles, sorted by category and collection. Capture catalogue : liste des ressources, filtres par type et par categorie, fiche ressource. FR Catalogue & collections
0:22-0:36 Every resource has its access model: free, read-only, per-unit purchase, included in a subscription, or private to your organisation. Capture fiche ressource : modele d'acces, prix, bouton de lecture ou d'achat. FR Free - read-only - purchase - subscription - private
0:36-0:50 The reader picks up where they left off: reading progress is saved, resource by resource. Capture lecteur en ligne, barre de progression reprise a la derniere page. FR Resume where you left off
0:50-1:00 Search finds a resource by its meaning, not only by its title. The Library is included: you pay for the platform, not the sector. Capture recherche semantique, puis CTA et logo. FR Search by meaning - module included

Call to action

Activate the Library module from the product site, at no pack cost.

Sound & music

Nappe douce, piano discret, 90 BPM. FR

Production notes

Module Library (api/app/Modules/Library) : types de document book, ebook, guide, manual, report, article ; modeles d'acces free, read_only, purchase, subscription, private (LibraryAccessModel). L'achat a l'unite reutilise le paiement Leopardo. La recherche est semantique (LibrarySemanticScorer). Ne pas promettre de pret physique ni de gestion d'emprunteurs : le module gere des ressources numeriques, pas des ouvrages papier. FR
