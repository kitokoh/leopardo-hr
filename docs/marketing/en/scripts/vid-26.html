Restaurant: the dinner rush, the evening margin (9:16 TikTok)

📱 Short formats & social9:16 ⏱ 35 s 🎯 Restaurant owners, floor managers, restaurant chains — TikTok, Reels, Shorts

Film objective

Decline the Restaurant vertical in native vertical format: show the dinner rush in the dining room and the evening margin, without a long product demonstration. Points to the 16:9 film VID-05 for the full version.

Hook (first 3 seconds)

« The dinner rush is over. Do you know what the service brought in? »

Breakdown

TimeVoice-over Shot / imageOn-screen text
