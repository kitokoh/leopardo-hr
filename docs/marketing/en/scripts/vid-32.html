Ride-hailing: the trip, the nearest driver, the margin (9:16 TikTok)

📱 Short formats & social9:16 ⏱ 35 s 🎯 Ride-hailing and taxi fleets, local mobility platforms - TikTok, Reels, Shorts

Film objective

Decline the ride-hailing vertical in native vertical format: the booking, the nearest-driver dispatch and the trip tracking, without a long product demonstration. Points to the 16:9 film VID-12 for the full version.

Hook (first 3 seconds)

« The customer calls. You look for who is closest. By hand. »

Breakdown

TimeVoice-over Shot / imageOn-screen text

0:05-0:12 Three calls for one trip. And the driver next door is still waiting. Cut rapide : doigt sur la carte, message vocal, chauffeur au volant qui hausse les epaules. FR One trip, three calls.

Call to action

Full film (16:9) in bio - activate the ride-hailing pack, with no pack fee.

Sound & music

Rythme urbain, 120 BPM, coupe nette sur le CTA. FR

Production notes

Format vertical natif : sous-titres brules obligatoires, texte dans les 60 % centraux (zone sure TikTok), aucun chiffre invente. Derive du film 16:9 VID-12 : reutiliser les rushes de standard et les captures d'ecran, ne pas re-tourner. Le pack VTC requiert le core geospatial `geo` (PostGIS) : ne pas promettre de dispatch temps reel si l'environnement de demonstration ne l'a pas active. Aucun nom de chauffeur ni plaque d'immatriculation reelle a l'ecran. FR
