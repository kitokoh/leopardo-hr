Leopardo — payroll and time tracking for your field teams (flagship film)

🎬 Solution overview16:9 ⏱ 90 s 🎯 Hands-on SME owners and accounting firms, Algeria first

Film objective

Make people understand in 90 seconds that Leopardo solves one precise problem: prove the hours and pay right. The business suite appears in the second half only, as an activatable catalogue, never as the hook. Master film from which the 30-second and 15-second cuts derive.

Hook (first 3 seconds)

« Your timesheets on paper. Your payroll on a spreadsheet. And by evening, an hours dispute nobody can settle. »

Breakdown

TimeVoice-over Shot / imageOn-screen text
0:00-0:10 Every morning your teams clock in on paper. Your payroll is calculated on a spreadsheet. And when an employee contests his hours, nobody can prove anything. Montage serre : carnet de pointage qui se remplit, tableur qui defile, deux personnes qui s'opposent devant un planning. FR Paper proves nothing.
0:10-0:22 Leopardo brings payroll and time tracking for your field teams into a single application. Les trois plans precedents se dissolvent dans une interface unique qui s'assemble. FR Payroll + time tracking. One app.
0:22-0:40 Clock-in comes from mobile, GPS or the ZKTeco biometric kiosk. When the network drops, the terminal keeps running and resyncs as soon as the link returns. Employe qui badine sur un kiosque, telephone en mode hors-ligne, badge de synchronisation qui passe au vert. FR Every hour is proven.
0:40-1:00 Algerian payroll applies: IRG, CNAS, attendance and leave variables pulled in, payslip generated as PDF, run locked once validated. Ecran de simulation de paie, puis bulletin PDF qui se genere, puis cadenas sur le run. FR Payroll is calculated, validated, locked.
1:00-1:12 The employee record, the contract, leave and documents are entered once and used everywhere. Creation d'un employe, puis le meme employe visible dans paie, pointage et conges. FR The employee record feeds everything else.
1:12-1:24 And because your business does not stop at payroll: accounting, CRM, and activatable business packs when you need them. Grille de modules qui s'allument un par un, hors du chemin principal. FR One core. 38 activatable modules.
1:24-1:30 Fourteen days free, no credit card. Set up in under thirty minutes. Logo, URL, bouton d'essai. FR 14-day trial — no card

Call to action

Try free for 14 days on kitokoh.github.io/leopardo-hr, or book a demo.

Sound & music

Percussion organique montante, 100-110 BPM, silence d'une seconde avant le CTA. FR

Production notes

Accroche de lancement : paie + pointage, Algerie d'abord (repositionnement 2026). La suite metier n'apparait qu'au beat 6, en catalogue. Les referentiels de paie sont en statut pilot : ne jamais dire « conformite validee ». Le film doit rester comprehensible sans son : chaque plan porte son texte a l'ecran. FR
