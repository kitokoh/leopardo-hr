Fleet & cameras: what rolls, what films

🏭 Verticals16:9 ⏱ 60 s 🎯 Companies with a vehicle fleet, sites to supervise, fleet managers

Film objective

Show the Fleet and Cameras modules: vehicles and assignments, due dates (insurance, roadworthiness) and alerts, supervised IP cameras with time-limited logged access, and deduplicated alerts.

Hook (first 3 seconds)

« A vehicle that breaks down is a route that stops. A silent camera is a doubt that lingers. »

Breakdown

TimeVoice-over Shot / imageOn-screen text
0:00-0:08 A vehicle that breaks down is a route that stops. A silent camera is a doubt that lingers. Vehicule au bord de la route, puis écran de supervision qui affiche une camera hors ligne. FR What must roll. What must film.
0:08-0:22 Fleet holds every vehicle: plate number, brand, mileage, assignment to a driver and a site. Capture liste des vehicules, fiche vehicule, affectation chauffeur. FR Vehicles & assignments
0:22-0:36 Insurance and roadworthiness have a due date. The system lists vehicles coming due and raises an alert before the date. Capture echeances (assurance, contrôle technique), liste des vehicules a echeance, alerte. FR Due dates & alerts
0:36-0:50 IP cameras are declared, supervised and viewed through a time-limited access link. Every access is logged. Capture supervision cameras, lien d'acces temporaire, journal des acces. FR Cameras & logged access
0:50-1:00 Camera alerts are deduplicated: a burst of motion produces a single alert. Fleet and Cameras are included: you pay for the platform, not the sector. Capture alerte camera (mouvement, personne, vehicule, franchissement, sabotage), puis CTA et logo. FR Deduplicated alerts - modules included

Call to action

Activate the Fleet and Cameras modules from the product site, at no pack cost.

Sound & music

Basse reguliere, nappe tendue, 100 BPM. FR

Production notes

Modules Fleet (api/app/Modules/Fleet) et Cameras (api/app/Modules/Cameras). Fleet : Vehicle (immatriculation, marque, kilometrage, assurance, contrôle technique, affectation chauffeur/site), VehicleAlert, VehicleMaintenance, VehicleTrip. Cameras : Camera (rtsp_url chiffre en base), CameraAccessToken (lien a durée limitee), CameraAccessLog (journal des acces), CameraAlert (types motion, person, vehicle, line_crossing, tamper ; sévérités info, warning, high, critical ; cycle open -> acknowledged -> resolved ; dedupliquee par alert_key unique par tenant). Ne pas afficher de flux vidéo réel d'un site client : utiliser un flux de démonstration. FR
