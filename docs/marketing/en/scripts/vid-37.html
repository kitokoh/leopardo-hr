VID-37

Solidarity fundraisers: the link, the donation, the payout

🏭 Verticals16:9 ⏱ 60 s 🎯 Associations, NGOs, mutuals, schools, civic-minded companies, community groups

Film objective

Show the Fundraising module: a fundraiser published through a short link, card and mobile money contributions, collection tracking, payout to the beneficiary.

Hook (first 3 seconds)

« A collection that lives in a notebook and a phone, nobody can verify it. »

Breakdown

TimeVoice-over Shot / imageOn-screen text
0:00-0:08 A collection that lives in a notebook and a phone, nobody can verify it. Table de collecte : un carnet de dons manuscrit, un téléphone qui sonne, une liste de noms a moitié remplie. FR Who gave what?
0:08-0:22 The fundraiser is published through a short link. You share it on WhatsApp, on social media, or as a QR code. Giving needs no account. Capture cagnotte : création, publication, lien public court, page de don avec montants suggeres. FR One link, one donation page
0:22-0:36 Contributions arrive by bank card or mobile money. The amount is bounded by the fundraiser, and the donation can stay anonymous. Capture contribution : choix du montant, choix du moyen (carte, mobile money), option anonyme, message de soutien. FR Card & mobile money
0:36-0:50 The collection is tracked live: amount raised, number of contributions, supporters wall. The owner also confirms cash or bank-transfer donations. Capture suivi : jauge de collecte, compteur de contributions, mur des soutiens, puis confirmation d'une contribution manuelle. FR Live tracking
0:50-1:00 The balance is paid out to the beneficiary, by mobile money or bank transfer, with a trace of every step. The Solidarity Fundraisers pack is included. Capture reversement : demande, traitement, statut paye, journal des étapes, puis CTA et logo. FR Traced payout - pack included

Call to action

Activate the Solidarity Fundraisers pack from the product site, at no pack cost.

Sound & music

Nappe chaleureuse, cordes discrètes, 92 BPM. FR

Production notes

Module Fundraising (api/app/Modules/Fundraising, spec docs/specifications/SOLUTION_FUNDRAISING.md). Cagnotte : Fundraiser (slug public stable, catégorie medical/education/emergency/community/project/other, objectif, montants suggeres, fenetre de collecte starts_at..ends_at, statut draft/active/paused/completed/closed/cancelled). Contributions : ContributionMethod (card, mobile_money, bank_transfer, cash), ContributionStatus (pending, completed, failed, refunded) ; passerelles Stripe (carte), CinetPay / PayDunya / Pvit (mobile money), et confirmation manuelle des especes / virements par le responsable. Reversements : FundraisingPayout (method mobile_money ou bank_transfer, statut requested/processing/paid/failed/cancelled). Reglages : méthodes activees, montants min/max, anonymat, messages, affichage des soutiens. IMPORTANT : le recu fiscal automatique n'existe pas encore (mentionne 'recu phase 2' dans le code) : ne jamais promettre que les recus partent seuls. Ne pas afficher de montant collecte réel ni de nombre de donateurs : données de démonstration uniquement. FR
