### English Version
**Hook:** Stop managing field teams with fragmented spreadsheets and scattered messages. 🐆

Meet **Leopardo** — the open-source business suite for field-based companies: HR & payroll, attendance, leave, CRM, accounting and operations, on web, mobile and kiosks. Built for multi-site operations, it brings everything into one unified, mobile-first suite.

**Key Highlights:**
🔹 **Smart Attendance**: biometric cloud bridge & GPS-fenced check-ins.
🔹 **Payroll preparation, multi-country**: payroll engine and export — country rules in pilot (21 countries referenced as of 2026-09-09), validate for your context.
🔹 **Mobile-first**: 7 native Flutter apps + a shared core package (measured 2026-09-18) for employees, managers and admins.
🔹 **Enterprise-grade foundations**: multi-tenant isolation, RBAC, and full OpenAPI support (798 documented endpoints as of 2026-09-18).

## 💡 Conseils de publication
1. **Visuel** : accompagnez ces posts d'une capture d'écran du dashboard ou de l'app mobile (voir dossier `assets`).
2. **Tagging** : sur LinkedIn, identifiez les membres clés de l'équipe et vos premiers partenaires.
3. **Engagement** : répondez aux premiers commentaires dans l'heure qui suit pour booster l'algorithme.
4. **Vérification avant publication** : relire `MESSAGE.md` (promesses interdites : conformité paie « validée », chiffres non datés, iOS disponible) et dater tout chiffre via `METRIQUES_VITRINE.md`.
