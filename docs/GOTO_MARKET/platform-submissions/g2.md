## Fiche de référencement professionnel

**Product Name**

**Seller Name**

**Product Tagline**  
The open-source, AI-native HR & Payroll OS for high-growth companies.

**Product Description**  
Leopardo RH is a comprehensive, open-source Human Resource Management (HRM) and Payroll system designed for companies with significant field operations and multiple sites. It centralizes employee lifecycle management, biometric attendance tracking, multi-country payroll preparation, and workforce analytics into a single, mobile-first platform. Built with a modular monolith architecture (DDD), it offers high-compliance data isolation (multi-tenancy) and seamless integration via an OpenAPI-compliant layer.

**Categories**  
Core HR, Payroll, Time & Attendance, Workforce Management, Employee Self Service.

**Key Features to Highlight**  
- **Unified HR Core:** Centralized employee records, contracts, and digital document management.
- **Smart Attendance:** Biometric cloud bridge (ZKTeco), GPS-fenced mobile check-in, and edge kiosk support.
- **Automated Payroll:** Multi-country engine (DZ, MA, FR, TR) with automated validation and PDF generation.
- **AI-Native Insights:** Predictive workforce analytics and anomaly detection for proactive management.
- **Enterprise-Grade Security:** RBAC, SSO (SAML/OIDC), and schema-isolated multi-tenancy.

**Target Market**  
Mid-Market (51-1000 employees), Enterprise (>1000 employees) in emerging markets and high-growth sectors.
