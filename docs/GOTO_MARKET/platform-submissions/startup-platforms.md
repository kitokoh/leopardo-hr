## Textes communs

**Nom** : Leopardo
**Pitch (1 phrase, FR)** : Leopardo est la suite métier des entreprises de terrain — RH & paie, pointage, absences, CRM, comptabilité et opérations, sur web, mobile et bornes.
**Pitch (1 phrase, EN)** : Leopardo is the business suite for field-based companies — HR & payroll, attendance, leave, CRM, accounting and operations, on web, mobile and kiosks.

**Description longue (EN, ~80 mots)** :
Leopardo is an open-source (MIT), multi-tenant business suite built for companies that
manage field teams across multiple sites: HR & payroll, attendance (QR, GPS, biometric
kiosks), leave, CRM, accounting and operations — on web, mobile and kiosks. Payroll rules
cover 21 countries (pilot status, measured 2026-09-09), with a focus on French-speaking
Africa, the Maghreb and Turkey. Self-hostable or SaaS. Tenant-activable vertical packs:
travel agencies, restaurants & delivery, fuel stations, schools.

**Catégories** : Business suite / ERP / Open Source / B2B SaaS
**Marchés** : Algérie, Maroc, Tunisie, Sénégal, Côte d'Ivoire, Turquie, France, Canada
**Business model** : open core — self-host gratuit (MIT) + SaaS par employé/mois + verticaux
**Stade** : produit construit, pilotes en cours — ne pas revendiquer de traction non sourcée.
