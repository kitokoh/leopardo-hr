One suite, every operation

Run the whole company, not just HR

Leopardo brings the tools field-based teams actually use into one multi-tenant platform — no glue code, no per-module vendors.

💰

HR & payroll

Employee records, contracts, leave and a mobile-first payroll engine with payroll rules for 21 countries (pilot status), validation workflow and payroll export.

🕒

Attendance & kiosks

Mobile check-in with GPS geofencing, biometric ZKTeco kiosks, and an offline edge mode that keeps clocking when the network doesn't.

📇

CRM & accounting, built in

Leads, pipeline, invoicing and receivables tracking live in the same suite as your workforce data — one login, one source of truth.

🏪

Business verticals

Dedicated flows for restaurants, gas stations, travel agencies and education — an evolving catalog that grows release by release.

🔌

Open API

A fully documented OpenAPI spec, webhooks and SDKs — 798 endpoints (measured 2026-09-18) ready to integrate with your stack.

🔐

Multi-tenant & self-hostable

Strict tenant isolation, RBAC and encrypted sensitive data — run it in the cloud or self-host the whole MIT-licensed stack.
